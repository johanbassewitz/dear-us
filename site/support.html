Help

Can't find your answer? Write to support@axiantlabs.dev. We usually reply within two days.

Getting started

How do we connect?
One of you creates the shared space in Settings → Partner sync and sends the invite code privately (in person is best). The other installs Dear Us, chooses Join my partner and pastes the code. Each of you uses your own account.
When does the free trial start?
When your partner joins. For 30 days everything is unlocked for both of you.
Can I use Dear Us on another PC (or later, my phone)?
Yes, with the same account. On the device you already use: Settings → Partner sync → Link my phone or another PC. On the new one: I already use Dear Us on another device. Everything comes over, encrypted.

Account & safety

I forgot my password.
Click Forgot your password? where you sign in. We email you a 6-digit code. Your memories aren't affected: they're locked with your space key, not your password.
What is the recovery phrase?
24 words shown when you create your space. They're the key to your memories. You need them to open an encrypted backup on a new PC. Write them on paper and keep them safe; we can't recover them for you.
How do backups work?
Settings → Backups → pick your OneDrive, Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox. Dear Us makes an encrypted, verified backup every day while it's open.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Partner sync → Delete my account. Your memories stay on your PC and your partner keeps your space. If you pay for Premium, cancel it first (Settings → Your plan → Manage subscription).
How do I stop sharing with my partner?
Settings → Partner sync → Disconnect from my partner. Nothing is shared from then on, in either direction. Everything already on your PC stays.
Cupid said something wrong or hurtful.
Click Report under the answer. Cupid's answers are written by AI (Google Gemini) and can be wrong; he's not a therapist or a doctor. If you're in danger, contact local emergency services.

Plans

What happens after the trial if we don't subscribe?
Nothing is lost or locked. Your memories keep syncing. Photos and videos get 100 MB of cloud a month (unlimited over your home Wi-Fi), Cupid gets 10 arrows a month, and you can keep 3 time capsules sealed at once.
Does one subscription cover both of us?
Yes. Either of you can buy it; it covers both of you on all your devices.
How do I pay?
Settings → Your plan → Get Premium. A secure checkout by Paddle, our reseller, opens in your browser (card, PayPal and more, depending on your country). When it's done, Dear Us switches to Premium for both of you within a few seconds.
I paid but don't see it.
Wait a minute and keep Dear Us open. Still nothing? Write to us with the email on your Paddle receipt.
How do I cancel Premium?
Settings → Your plan → Manage subscription → Cancel. Premium stays until the end of the period you paid for. You can also manage it from the link in your Paddle receipt email, or at paddle.net.
Can I get a refund?
Yes, within 14 days. See the refund policy.
