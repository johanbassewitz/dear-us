4. Sharing with your partner

Dear Us shares your memories only with the person you invite with your invite code, and with your own linked devices. Only invite someone who wants to be connected with you. Either of you can disconnect at any time (Settings → Partner sync → Disconnect from my partner). From then on nothing more is shared, but what was already shared stays on the other person's devices; it can't be taken back remotely.
